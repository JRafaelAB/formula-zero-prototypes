Apex Line

The zero is a circuit seen from above, and the red slash is the racing line that clips both apexes. That also turns the symbol into a slashed zero (Ø), the typographic zero used on timing screens and code. It is the most modern direction: built for app icons, overlays and streaming, and it needs no letters to be recognised.

Geometry

The ring is a stadium shape (two semicircles on a 13-unit stroke) skewed by 14°. The racing line is a 10-unit band at 51°, with a 6-unit gap cut out of the ring on each side so it reads as a track crossing.

Clear space

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is the height of the counter (the hole) inside the 0. No other graphics, type or edges can enter this zone.
