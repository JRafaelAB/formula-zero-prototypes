Velocity

Velocity reads as one fast stroke. The italic F and the rounded zero share a 30° slant, and two speed lines trail the F like a slipstream. It is the most direct answer to "as close to F1 as possible", but it is built from original geometry: a monogram, not a modified F1 wordmark.

Logo suite

Use the primary lockup wherever there is room. Switch to the stacked version for square spaces, and to the symbol for avatars, icons and small sizes.

02

Construction & clear space

xxxx

Geometry

Every stroke sits on a 30° italic axis, and the F and the 0 use the same 12-unit stroke. The speed lines are a third of the cap height and step back by 8 units, as if fading into the distance.

Clear space

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is the height of the counter (the hole) inside the 0. No other graphics, type or edges can enter this zone.
