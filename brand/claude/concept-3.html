Grid Crest

Grid Crest treats Formula Zero as a club with a history: a shield like the ones on team kits and trophies, a checkered-flag crown, and an italic F0 in the middle. The chevron at the tip points down to the grid. It is the most emblematic direction, built for merch, team kits, season badges and champion trophies.

Geometry

The shield is drawn on a 104 × 112 grid with an 8° lean. The checkered crown uses 6-unit squares, the F0 letters share the shield’s lean and a 12-unit stroke, and the chevron mirrors the shield tip at 45% scale.

Clear space

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is the height of the counter (the hole) inside the 0. No other graphics, type or edges can enter this zone.
