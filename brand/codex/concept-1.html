02 / Geometry

Built on
precision.

The F and 0 share a 12° forward cut. The zero's counter stays open to a minimum 4 unit wall; the three exit bars step down by 11 units.

Construction grid / 8-unit modules
Clear space: X

Keep at least the height of the f’s top bar free on all sides. Use the same measurement around sponsor marks and container edges.

Optical alignment

Align to the visual mass of the mark, not the SVG canvas. Preserve the designed counters, gap and accent.

08 / Usage rules

Keep the
line clean.

Protect the original proportions, contrast and clear space. Supply the proper color artwork for each background.

Do / preserve the artwork

Use the supplied proportions and give it room to breathe.

Don’t / stretch

Scaling on one axis changes the geometry and damages legibility.

Don’t / rotate

The built-in motion angle is enough; keep the baseline level.

Don’t / lose contrast

Never place pale artwork on a similarly pale surface.
