THE IDEA

A single slanted plate gives the monogram a native place in racing culture. The white F0 is drawn with broad apertures and balanced padding, so it remains legible on a helmet or a 16-pixel icon.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The number plate is an emblem of competition, scaled from chassis to scoreboard. It needs no extra crest or decoration.

11 / USE

Keep it sharp

Do

  • Use the supplied SVG geometry at every size.
  • Keep one X of clear space around the symbol.
  • Use high-contrast approved color states.
  • Prioritize the symbol where the full lockup is too small.

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate or redraw the F0.
  • Add outlines, glows or gradients to the master mark.
  • Place the mark over busy detail without a solid field.
  • Recreate another racing organization’s logo.
